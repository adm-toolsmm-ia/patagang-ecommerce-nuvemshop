## Analysis Protocol

### Step 1: Analyst Reviews
@analyst (Alex) reads legacy frameworks and documents findings:
- Location: `.aiox-core/archived/LEGACY_INSIGHTS.md` (created during Phase 3)
- Focus: Valuable patterns, constraints, design decisions
- Outcome: Categorized insights (valuable/deprecated/partial)

### Step 2: Architect Evaluates
@architect (Aria) reviews findings and assesses AIOX integration:
- Decision: Migrate to AIOX or archive as historical
- Location: Individual `.claude/rules/` or `.aiox-core/data/` files
- Outcome: AIOX enrichment without structure changes

### Step 3: Agents Consume
Agents (dev, qa, architect, etc.) use enriched memories:
- Location: `.aiox-core/development/agents/{agent}/MEMORY.md`
- Method: Pattern integration (no framework changes)
- Outcome: AIOX agents benefit from prior learnings
